Partnerships & Giving Back

Creating the Shift Through Meaningful Partnerships

Great experiences don't happen because of places.
They happen because of people.

At 2 Degrees, we believe the most meaningful global experiences are created through authentic partnerships.

We bring together schools, Indigenous communities, governments, businesses, nonprofits, social enterprises, cultural organizations, educators, and local changemakers to create experiences that inspire learning, strengthen leadership, and create lasting impact.

Whether you're looking to share your expertise, host a visiting delegation, collaborate on a community initiative, or create new opportunities for learning and connection, we'd love to build something meaningful together.

Because when passionate people come together with a shared purpose, extraordinary things happen.
