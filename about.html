About 2 Degrees

We travel differently.

Delivering global experiences, travel planning, and business event services for international clients since the 1990s.

Learn. Lead. Impact.

2 Degrees Travel has been delivering global experiences, travel planning, and business event services for international clients since the 1990s. With strong local partnerships, international relationships, and group safety protocols, 2 Degrees Travel creates customized, purpose-driven travel experiences that make long-term impact.

Our philosophy

Travel Differently

At 2 Degrees, we don't just plan travel — we create opportunities to learn, lead, and make a difference. By bringing together schools, governments, organizations, cultural groups, and business partners, we create immersive experiences that inspire curiosity, strengthen relationships, and leave a lasting legacy.

Because one experience has the power to change a perspective, and one perspective has the power to change the world.

Our people

Meet the team

Kim Carvajal

Founder & Managing Director

Kim Carvajal is the visionary behind 2 Degrees Travel and the driving force behind our mission to create transformational, real-world learning experiences. As Founder and Managing Director, Kim leads with purpose, shaping the company's direction, values, and growing global impact.

With a background in international business management, she blends strategic leadership with hands-on program design to empower young changemakers. Kim believes deeply in the transformational power of travel — creating opportunities that spark curiosity, build leadership, and inspire who students become along the way.

Beyond her work, Kim is a proud mom and an avid adventurer, always exploring new destinations or hiking in her local mountains.

Laurie-Ann Tao

Program Manager

Laurie-Ann Tao is a passionate educator and advocate for inclusive, community-centred learning. As Program Manager at 2 Degrees Travel, she brings experience from Ontario and BC's public, private, and alternative education systems, supporting youth from diverse and vulnerable backgrounds.

Her approach combines academic insight with heart-centred leadership. Through experiential and outdoor education, she uses nature to foster self-discovery, resilience, and growth. With a background in education, the arts, and ongoing counselling studies, she supports youth with both creativity and care.

Outside of her work, Laurie-Ann leads rock climbing adventures in Squamish, practises yoga, gardens, and actively supports her community.

Ella Stewart

Marketing & Communications Coordinator

Ella Stewart is the creative force behind how 2 Degrees Travel connects with the world. As Marketing & Communications Coordinator, she shapes every message and story, ensuring the mission reaches students, families, educators, and global partners with clarity and heart.

A passionate storyteller, Ella blends creativity with strategy through engaging content, outreach, and digital experiences. With a strong communications background, she brings a sharp eye for detail, collaboration, and a commitment to meaningful storytelling.

Outside of work, Ella enjoys hiking, camping, exploring new places, and spending time with her two beloved dogs.
