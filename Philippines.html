Palawan, Philippines

Some lessons can't be taught in a classroom. They must be experienced.

Imagine your students stepping into vibrant communities, collaborating with local leaders, learning alongside Indigenous and cultural communities, and discovering a deeper understanding of themselves and the world around them.

This isn't simply a trip to the Philippines.

It's an immersive leadership experience designed to inspire curiosity, build confidence, cultivate global citizenship, and create meaningful connections that last a lifetime.

Students don't just visit the Philippines. They experience it.

Plan your journey

Why the Philippines?

Warmth, hospitality, and resilience — an environment where visitors quickly feel welcomed and connected.

Its unique blend of vibrant culture, resilient communities, breathtaking landscapes, and deep-rooted hospitality creates the ideal setting for students to grow both personally and professionally.

Throughout the journey, students engage with local schools, organizations, educators, community leaders, and Indigenous knowledge keepers while gaining firsthand insight into sustainability, social innovation, education, environmental stewardship, and community development.

Every interaction becomes an opportunity to broaden perspectives and deepen understanding.

The experience

What Students Will Experience

Cultural Immersion

Experience Filipino traditions, cuisine, music, language, history, and everyday life through authentic local connections.

Community Development

Through collective fundraising, hands-on experience building an eco-home, and guided reflections, participants take part in life-changing moments that make a difference and last a lifetime.

Community Engagement

Work alongside local schools and communities while learning the importance of collaboration, service, and reciprocal relationships.

Environmental Stewardship

Learn about ocean conservation, sustainability initiatives, community leadership, biodiversity, and environmental responsibility.

Personal Growth

Step outside your comfort zone, build independence, embrace new perspectives, and discover yourself, your contribution, and your place in this world.

Our approach

Learning Beyond the Classroom

At 2 Degrees, we believe experiential learning creates the deepest understanding.

Rather than simply hearing about global issues, students experience them firsthand. Rather than studying leadership, they practice it. Rather than observing another culture, they become part of it.

The result is greater confidence, empathy, cultural intelligence, resilience, and a broader perspective that students carry long after they return home.

The difference

What Makes This Experience Different?

It's travel with purpose. Education through experience.

Leadership through connection. Impact through action.
